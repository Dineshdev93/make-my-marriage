## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a poised, serene, and exquisitely structured wedding planning environment. Drawing from bespoke editorial paper craft and quiet luxury publications, the interface establishes an atmosphere of quiet confidence, timeless romance, and immaculate order.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Tone & Voice:** Warm, calm, reassuring, and exacting. Prose is deliberate and uncluttered. Financial or operational jargon (e.g., "ledger", "manifest", "fiscal", "liquid contingency") is strictly prohibited. Exclamation points are entirely omitted to preserve an unhurried, serene dignity.
- **Visual Discipline:** Zero decorative distractions. There are no illustrations, florals, mandalas, emojis, stock photography clichés, or synthetic gradients. Hierarchy is conveyed entirely through spatial rhythm, refined serif displays, and hairline stone borders.
- **Cultural Grounding:** Uncompromising precision with localized nuances, notably Indian financial formatting (rupee symbols and Lakh/Crore numbering conventions: e.g., ₹15,00,000).

## Colors

The color palette centers around an unyielding rule of warm ivory and deep burgundy wine, completely eliminating any trace of peach, cream, pink tint, evergreen, yellow, orange, gold, or bright saturated red.

### Surface and Canvas Architecture
- **Page Background (`#F7F4EE`):** Applied uniformly across every section, canvas, and viewport ground. A strictly neutral warm ivory with no pinkish or peach cast.
- **Raised Surfaces & Cards (`#FCFBF8`):** Sits slightly forward from the canvas, bounded by a 1px hairline border (`#DDD6CA`).
- **Accent Bands:**
  - High-impact CTA Bands: Deep Wine (`#4A1426`).
  - Global Footers: Anchor Dark Wine (`#2E0F1B`).

### Content & Functional Colors
- **Headings & Body Text (`#23211D`):** Rich charcoal. Headings must always be rendered in charcoal, never wine.
- **Secondary & Muted Copy (`#6B665E`):** Soft warm charcoal for timestamps, captions, and structural secondary cues.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#6B1D34`):** Deep burgundy wine. Strictly isolated to primary action fills, key focal numerals (e.g., countdown clocks, remaining budget values), active navigation tokens, and inline text links. Hover shifts to `#55162A`. Text atop primary wine is always warm ivory `#F7F4EE`.
- **Feedback & Semantics:**
  - Success Accent: Sage (`#9FB09A`), applied exclusively to completed states and reconciled milestones.
  - Informational & Warning Accent: Slate Blue (`#4A6074`).
  - Error States: Deep wine text (`#6B1D34`) paired with an understated stroke icon. Saturated alerts and bright reds are explicitly banned.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is grounded in two typefaces that together produce an editorial, poised layout:

- **Headings & Key Numerals (Newsreader):** Handled in light to regular weights (`300` and `400`) with tight tracking. Display numerals (event dates, countdown figures, and financial summaries) are set in Newsreader and tinted in deep wine (`#6B1D34`), while section headers are strictly set in charcoal (`#23211D`).
- **Body & Functional UI (Inter):** Handled within the 14px to 16px range with a comfortable line-height ratio of 1.6 (`body-lg` at 26px, `body-md` at 22px). Ensures crisp legibility for complex task management, guest tallies, and ceremonial schedules.
- **Financial Typographic Treatment:** Indian number format must be strictly observed across all budget, cost, and booking modules (e.g., ₹2,50,000 and ₹1,20,00,000 rather than Western thousands intervals).

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow a structured fluid grid calibrated for editorial pacing:

- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column grid, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, centered container with a maximum width of `1200px`, and outer margins of 2.5rem (`40px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column grid, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, outer margins of 2rem (`32px`).
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, outer margins of 1.25rem (`20px`).

Vertical pacing prioritizes whitespace over structural enclosure. Sections breathe with generous top and bottom padding (`space-xl` to double `space-xl`), allowing cards and typographic columns to act as clean architectural blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through delicate surface tinting and structural hairline framing rather than heavy physical dropshadows:

- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Every card, container, input, and floating sheet utilizes a 1px hairline border in `#DDD6CA`.
- **Soft Low Shadows:** Floating dropdowns, contextual popovers, and elevated modal dialogues use an ultra-diffused, barely perceptible shadow tinted with charcoal: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(35, 33, 29, 0.04)`.
- **Flat Surface Separation:** In-page cards rest on Raised Ivory (`#FCFBF8`) directly over Page Background (`#F7F4EE`) without cast shadows. Depth remains quiet and non-tactile.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances soft tactile corners with disciplined rectangular framing:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` token / 1rem equivalent) combined with the 1px `#DDD6CA` border.
- **Controls & Form Inputs:** Exactly 10px radius across all buttons, input fields, dropdown toggles, and contextual action chips.
- **Micro Tags & Status Badges:** 6px radius to maintain a tailored, stationery-cut profile.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:**
  - Fill: `#6B1D34` (Deep Wine).
  - Text: `#F7F4EE` (Warm Ivory).
  - Hover: `#55162A`.
  - Border: None.
  - Border Radius: 10px.
  - Height & Padding: 44px height; horizontal padding of 20px. Font: `label-md`.
- **Secondary Action:**
  - Fill: Transparent.
  - Text: `#23211D` (Charcoal).
  - Border: 1px hairline `#DDD6CA`.
  - Hover: Background subtly tints to `#FCFBF8`, border shifts to `#6B1D34`.
  - Border Radius: 10px.
- **Tertiary / Text Action:**
  - Fill: Transparent; no border.
  - Text: `#6B1D34` with an underline offset of 3px.

### Cards & Modules
- Fill: `#FCFBF8`.
- Border: 1px hairline `#DDD6CA`.
- Border Radius: 16px.
- Shadow: None by default; reliance purely on the border and ivory tone.
- Padding: 24px (`space-lg`) on mobile, 32px on desktop.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Surface: `#FCFBF8`.
- Border: 1px hairline `#DDD6CA`.
- Border Radius: 10px.
- Text Color: `#23211D` (Placeholder: `#6B665E` at 65% opacity).
- Height: 44px.
- Focus State: Border transitions cleanly to `#6B1D34` with no secondary outer glow.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- **Completed / Reconciled:** Sage fill (`rgba(159, 176, 154, 0.15)`), sage text (`#475943`), 1px border (`#9FB09A` at 40%).
- **Informational / In Progress:** Slate blue fill (`rgba(74, 96, 116, 0.12)`), slate blue text (`#4A6074`), 1px border (`#4A6074` at 30%).
- **Error State:** Deep wine text (`#6B1D34`) accompanied by an understated 14px line icon. No saturated red boxes or warning fills.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Size: 18px × 18px.
- Unchecked: `#FCFBF8` background with 1px border `#DDD6CA`. Radius: 4px for checkboxes, 50% for radios.
- Checked: Fill `#6B1D34` with `#F7F4EE` checkmark or dot.

### Lists & Tables
- Dividers: Single 1px border-bottom `#DDD6CA`.
- Table Header: Charcoal (`#23211D`), `label-sm`, uppercase with `0.06em` tracking.
- Row States: No alternating zebra fills. Hover triggers an ivory shift to `#FCFBF8`.

### CTA Band & Footer
- **CTA Band:** Solid background `#4A1426`, text `#F7F4EE`, heading in `Newsreader` regular, button inverted (warm ivory fill `#F7F4EE` with `#4A1426` text).
- **Footer:** Background `#2E0F1B`, muted text `#DDD6CA`, hairline borders `#4A1426`.
